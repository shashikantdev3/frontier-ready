## Before you begin

### Who can earn the badge?

Engineers at a company enrolled in the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program. You need a work email for the Partner Skilling Hub, because personal addresses are not supported. See [Before you start](/before-you-start/).

### How long does it take?

LevelUp estimates about 86 hours if you take every course. {% unofficial %} The courses are optional, so it depends on what you already know. I passed the three exams in 14 days and finished in about four weeks, with a strong Microsoft AI background. See [how I did it](/#how-i-did-it).

### What does it cost?

The three exams are paid. Associate and expert exams are typically US$165 each, charged in local currency. Ask your company about vouchers first: partners in the Enterprise Skills Initiative get 50% off. See [Milestone 1](/milestone-1/#book-and-sit-an-exam).

### Do I need my own Azure subscription?

Only for the hands-on labs on the exam pages. A free Azure account gives a $200 credit for 30 days. Delete every resource group when you finish a lab.

## During the badge

### Does the order matter?

Set up your accounts first. That part matters most. After that, the suggested order is GH-300, AI-103, AB-100, then the Project Ready assessments, then Hypervelocity Engineering. See [the three milestones](/#the-three-milestones).

### I already hold AI-102. Do I need AI-103?

No, an active AI-102 is accepted in its place. AI-102 retired on 30 June 2026 and can't be renewed, so check its expiry date in your Microsoft Learn transcript. See [AI-103](/ai-103/#exam-day).

### What if I fail an exam?

You can retake it after 24 hours. After that, you wait 14 days between attempts, with at most five attempts in 12 months.

### What if I fail an assessment?

You get up to 10 attempts at each Project Ready and Hypervelocity Engineering assessment, and you need 80% to pass. {% unofficial %} Review the module you were weakest on before you try again.

## After the badge

### How do I know it has been issued?

Badges seen so far are issued on Credly, with Microsoft as the issuer. Watch your work email. See [Getting the badge](/badge/).

### Does the badge expire?

LevelUp doesn't say. Your certifications do: associate and expert certifications need a free online renewal every year, and the GitHub Copilot certification lasts two years.

### What happens if I leave my company?

Your certifications live in your Microsoft Learn profile. If that profile has a personal account on it, you keep it when you leave. If it only has a work account, you can lose it. See [Before you start](/before-you-start/#get-your-microsoft-learn-profile-ready).

### Something is missing. Who do I ask?

Work through the checklist on [Getting the badge](/badge/#if-something-does-not-show-up) first. Then use the "I need Help" button on LevelUp, or Partner Center support for linking problems.

### Is this guide official?

No. It is an independent guide written by someone who earned the badge. Every page links to its sources, and facts that only come from an unofficial source are marked {% unofficial %}.
