## The three exams at a glance

| Exam | What it covers | Length | Learn open during the exam? |
|---|---|---|---|
| {% stamp "GH-300" %} [GitHub Copilot](/gh-300/) | Using Copilot well and safely, and its admin settings | 100 min | No |
| {% stamp "AI-103" %} [Developing AI Apps and Agents on Azure](/ai-103/) | Building AI apps and agents with Microsoft Foundry | 120 min | Yes |
| {% stamp "AB-100" %} [Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect](/ab-100/) | Planning, designing and deploying agent solutions across Copilot, Copilot Studio, Dynamics 365 and Foundry | Not stated yet | Yes |

All three need **700** to pass.

{% note "Microsoft Learn in the exam" %}
In associate and expert exams you can open Microsoft Learn, except Q&A and practice assessments. The timer keeps running while you read, so use it for one or two lookups, not every question.
{% endnote %}

## Take them in this order

1. **GH-300 first.** It covers a tool you may already use, and passing it lets you link your Microsoft Learn profile in Partner Center.
2. **AI-103 second.** It is the broadest technical exam, with five topic areas from agents and search to vision, speech and documents.
3. **AB-100 last.** It asks you to choose between Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, Dynamics 365 and Foundry, which is easier once AI-103 has taught you Foundry.

{% must %}
- AB-100 alone does not give you the Expert certification. You also need one Associate certification from Microsoft's list, and **AI-103 is on that list**. So passing AI-103 first covers both.
- If you hold an **active AI-102**, it is accepted in place of AI-103. AI-102 retired on 30 June 2026 and can no longer be renewed, so check its expiry date in your Microsoft Learn transcript.
{% endmust %}

{% more "Is the badge based on the AB-100 exam or the Expert certification?" %}
Microsoft's partner blog lists "AB-100 (Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect)" without saying whether the exam alone is enough. If you follow the order above, you will have both, so it does not matter.
{% endmore %}

## Book and sit an exam

- **Book** from the exam's page on Microsoft Learn, through Pearson VUE. Microsoft recommends using a personal Microsoft account.
- **Price** depends on your country and is shown when you book. Ask your company whether it has exam vouchers before you pay.
- **Online or test centre.** For an online exam you need a walled room with a closed door, a clear desk and nobody else in the room. Run Pearson VUE's system test on the same computer and in the same place you will use.
- **ID.** Bring a current government ID. The name must exactly match the name on your Microsoft certification profile.
- **Check in** from 30 minutes before your start time. It takes about 15 minutes.
- **Try the exam screen** before your first exam: GitHub's [GH-300 sandbox](https://GHCertDemo.starttest.com), or Microsoft's [exam sandbox](https://aka.ms/examdemo) for AI-103 and AB-100.

{% more "Exam vouchers" %}
In September 2026 a Microsoft community manager wrote that there is no free voucher program for all partners. Partners in the Enterprise Skills Initiative (ESI) get 50% discounted vouchers. Microsoft also runs limited-time offers from time to time, such as one in May 2026 tied to LevelUp prep assessments. Ask your company's partner or training team before you pay.
{% endmore %}

## After you pass

- Microsoft associate and expert certifications **expire every year**. You renew them with a **free** online assessment on Microsoft Learn.
- The GitHub Copilot certification is valid for **2 years**. GitHub is moving to Microsoft's renewal process, and certifications that expire before it is ready get 6 extra months.
- Your new certification can take up to 10 days to appear in Partner Center.
