## What the badge is

{% note "Why your company cares" %}
To earn Microsoft's Frontier Partner specialization, a partner company needs five people with this badge. So your badge counts for your team too.
{% endnote %}

Microsoft gives the FTE badge to individual engineers, not to companies. It shows that you can design, build and deploy AI apps and agents on the Microsoft stack.

To earn it, you prove three things:

1. You hold three certifications.
2. You can do hands-on project work in key areas, such as Copilot, Fabric and Agent 365.
3. You know two delivery patterns: the AI Center of Excellence model and the Hypervelocity Engineering model.


## The three milestones

Do them in this order. Each one builds on the one before.

<ol class="milestones">
<li><div>
<h3>Set up your accounts</h3>
<p>Create a Partner Skilling Hub account with your work email. Get your Microsoft Learn profile ready. If you skip this, your completions may never reach your badge record.</p>
</div></li>
<li><div>
<h3>Certifications</h3>
<p>Pass {% stamp "GH-300" %} GitHub Copilot, {% stamp "AI-103" %} Developing AI Apps and Agents on Azure, and {% stamp "AB-100" %} Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect. If you already hold an active {% stamp "AI-102" %}, it counts in place of AI-103.</p>
</div></li>
<li><div>
<h3>Project Ready courses</h3>
<p>Pass five course assessments with 80% or more. The assessments cover Microsoft IQ, Work IQ, agentic AI, Fabric IQ and Agent 365. A course goes with each one to prepare you, but only the assessments are required. {% unofficial %}</p>
</div></li>
<li><div>
<h3>Hypervelocity Engineering</h3>
<p>Pass the Hypervelocity Engineering Framework assessment. The AI Center of Excellence and industry modules are optional. {% unofficial %}</p>
</div></li>
</ol>

{% more "Why some facts say \"unofficial\"" %}
Microsoft's own pages confirm the three certifications, the course work and the two delivery patterns. The exact course names, the 80% pass mark and which modules are optional come from LevelUp, the training site that runs the course list. Treat those details as likely but not guaranteed, and check the course page before you start.
{% endmore %}
