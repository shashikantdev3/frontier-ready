## What the badge is

{% note "Why your company cares" %}
To earn Microsoft's Frontier Partner specialization, a partner company needs five people with this badge. So your badge counts for your team too.
{% endnote %}

Microsoft gives the FTE badge to individual engineers, not to companies. It shows that you can design, build and deploy AI apps and agents on the Microsoft stack.

To earn it, you prove three things:

1. You hold three certifications.
2. You can do hands-on project work in key areas, such as Copilot, Fabric and Agent 365.
3. You know two delivery patterns: the AI Center of Excellence model and the Hypervelocity Engineering model.

| **Who can earn it** | Engineers at a company enrolled in the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program. You need a work email for the Partner Skilling Hub. |
| **Total time** | About **86 hours** if you take every course, by LevelUp's estimate. {% unofficial %} The courses are optional, so people who already know the stack need less. |
| **Exam fees** | Associate and expert exams are typically **US$165** each, charged in local currency. In India the exam page has shown about **₹4,865** plus tax. {% unofficial %} Ask about company vouchers before you pay. |
| **Your own Azure** | Optional, for the exam labs. A free account gives a $200 credit for 30 days. Delete what you create. |

## The three milestones

Do them in this order. Each one builds on the one before.

<ol class="milestones">
<li><div>
<h3>Set up your accounts</h3>
<p>Create a Partner Skilling Hub account with your work email. Get your Microsoft Learn profile ready. If you skip this, your completions may never reach your badge record.</p>
</div></li>
<li><div>
<h3>Certifications</h3>
<p>Pass {% stamp "GH-300" %} GitHub Copilot, {% stamp "AI-103" %} Developing AI Apps and Agents on Azure, and {% stamp "AB-100" %} Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect. If you already hold an active {% stamp "AI-102" %}, it counts in place of AI-103.</p>
</div></li>
<li><div>
<h3>Project Ready courses</h3>
<p>Pass five course assessments with 80% or more. The assessments cover Microsoft IQ, Work IQ, agentic AI, Fabric IQ and Agent 365. A course goes with each one to prepare you, but only the assessments are required. {% unofficial %}</p>
</div></li>
<li><div>
<h3>Hypervelocity Engineering</h3>
<p>Pass the Hypervelocity Engineering Framework assessment. The AI Center of Excellence and industry modules are optional. {% unofficial %}</p>
</div></li>
</ol>

{% more "Why some facts say \"unofficial\"" %}
Microsoft's own pages confirm the three certifications, the course work and the two delivery patterns. The exact course names, the 80% pass mark and which modules are optional come from LevelUp, the training site that runs the course list. Treat those details as likely but not guaranteed, and check the course page before you start.
{% endmore %}

## How I did it

These dates come from my own work log. I already held an active AI-102 and had about two and a half years of hands-on work with Copilot Studio and Azure AI. So treat my 14 days as a pace for someone with that background, not a target.

<ol class="timeline">
<li><time datetime="2026-09-22">Day 1, 22 Sep</time> <span>Enrolled in the Frontier programme and booked the exams. One hour.</span></li>
<li><time datetime="2026-09-25">Day 4, 25 Sep</time> <span>Studied for GH-300 and AB-100 together. Four and a half hours.</span></li>
<li><time datetime="2026-09-28">Day 7, 28 Sep</time> <span>Sat <span class="stamp">GH-300</span> and <span class="stamp">AB-100</span> on the same day, with half an hour of revision before the first and an hour between them. Passed both.</span></li>
<li><time datetime="2026-09-29">Day 8, 29 Sep</time> <span>Four hours of GitHub Copilot training on LevelUp.</span></li>
<li><time datetime="2026-09-30">Days 9 and 10</time> <span>Three hours of AI-103 training each day, plus an hour of exam prep each evening. Took my first two LevelUp assessments, an hour each.</span></li>
<li><time datetime="2026-10-05">Day 14, 5 Oct</time> <span>Sat <span class="stamp">AI-103</span> at a test centre. Passed. All three certifications done.</span></li>
<li><time datetime="2026-10-07">Days 16 to 23</time> <span>The rest of the assessments, about one a day: two hours of course work, then the one-hour assessment.</span></li>
</ol>

{% mine "My three tips" %}
1. **Set up the accounts on day one.** It took me an hour on my first day, before any study. Everything after that depends on these accounts being linked the right way.
2. **Book the exams before you feel ready.** A date in the calendar sets the pace. If you already use Copilot every day, GH-300 and AB-100 can share a day, with a revision break between them.
3. **One assessment a day.** Prepare for a couple of hours, take the assessment while it is fresh, and stop. An hour of assessment after a full day of work is enough.
{% endmine %}
